Featured Illustration Case Study

Lord Beerus

Beerus struck me as a weird character. I wanted to see how I could draw this my way. I wanted to capture the calm confidence that makes him such an unforgettable character while pushing the lighting and color to create something bold.

Watch The ProcessExplore Illustration

Project Overview

Drawing Beerus My Way

I started with the sketch, worked through the line art, built up the color, and finished the piece with the lighting and glow that gave it the bold look I wanted.

Project Type:Character Illustration
Subject:Lord Beerus from Dragon Ball Z Super
Process:Sketch → Line Art → Color Blocking → Rendering → Final Illustration
Tools Used:Adobe Fresco

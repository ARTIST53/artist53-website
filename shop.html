Shop questions

Can I order something now?

Yes. The Vector Lounge Volume 1 is $35 with $6 US shipping. Buy through PayPal on the book page. Books ship within 1–2 days after purchase, and the included coloring PDF is emailed within 24 hours. ATLANTA BRAVE and GAME OVER are currently accepting early-access interest only, without payment or a guaranteed reservation.

Can I preorder ATLANTA BRAVE or GAME OVER?

Not yet. Join the early-access list above to be notified when pricing, availability and shipping timelines are confirmed. Signing up does not charge you or reserve a print.

Will the PDFs be physical products?

The planned PDF collection is digital. Each future listing will explain the file format, page count and permitted use. Printed products will be listed separately.

Where can I ask about the shop?

Email justin@artist53.com with your question or the collection you want to see first.

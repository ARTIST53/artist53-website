Reason For The Illustration

Atlanta Became Part Of My Story

I came to Georgia in 1996 from Columbia, South Carolina. I was 16 years old, following the tail end of my father’s journey with Norfolk Southern Railway and stepping into a new life.

Atlanta became part of my story. My brother and I bumped Southernplayalisticadillacmuzik until I popped the tape. Over the years, the city gave me experiences and memories through its art, music, events, culture and people.

Atlanta Brave is my visual interpretation of that journey, coming to Atlanta, experiencing the city, enduring its storms and still standing.

The piece incorporates visual references to Atlanta and Georgia, including the city’s interstate system, music and cultural influences, while the central figure embodies strength, survival and the spirit of the city.

Reason For The Illustration

Atlanta Became Part Of My Story

I came to Georgia in 1996 by way of Columbia, South Carolina. A young 16 year old ready to blaze a trail on the tail end of my father’s journey (Norfolk Southern Railways) for an upgraded life for us. My brother and I bumped that Southernplayalistic until I popped the tape! Atlanta has given me numerous experiences and memories via art, music, events and people. I’ve managed to Brave the storm that this mecca has had to offer and I’m still standing.

Illustration done in Adobe Draw on the iPad Pro. Final illustration rendered in Adobe Illustrator.

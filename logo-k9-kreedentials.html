Logo Identity Case Study

K-9 Kreedentials

A bold identity system created for a dog training, breeding and grooming business, built around an illustrated canine mark and a strong black-and-white visual foundation.

View The ProjectBack To Logos

Project Overview

A Working-Dog Identity With Authority

The goal was to create a logo that felt disciplined, dependable and immediately connected to professional dog services. The illustrated dog portrait gives the brand personality, while the heavy typography, banner shape and stars create a badge-like identity that can work across apparel, signage, social media and printed materials.

Primary Identity

Built To Work In Black Or White

The strongest version of the identity holds together without relying on color. That gives the mark flexibility for uniforms, vinyl, signage, embroidery and one-color printing.

Color Development

Exploring The Final Palette

Blue, purple and gold combinations were explored to add a premium, professional tone while keeping the illustrated dog and bold structure readable.

Concept Exploration

Testing Multiple Badge Directions

Several layout directions were tested, including ribbon, shield and angled badge structures. This process helped identify the version with the strongest balance between illustration, typography and service messaging.

Reference To Illustration

Turning The Real Dog Into The Brand

The final illustrated dog was developed from the client's real animal reference, giving the identity a personal and recognizable foundation rather than using a generic stock image.

Sketches & Development

Finding The Visual Language

The identity began with several visual directions, including symbolic hands, roots, typography, African-inspired pattern work and the raised megaphone concept.

02 / Proofing

Chosen Direction

Narrowing It Down

Two developed directions were presented side by side. The megaphone concept moved forward, giving the identity a stronger sense of voice, leadership, energy and movement.

The selected direction combines the illustrated raised megaphone with the Manhood Basic Training wordmark and pattern system as one complete mark.

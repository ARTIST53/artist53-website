Logo Identity Case Study

Manhood Basic Training

A look at the development of the Manhood Basic Training identity—from early concepts and client proofing through the selected direction, final mark, complete logo system and individual logo variations.

01 / Exploration

Sketches & Development

Finding The Visual Language

The identity began with several different visual directions, including symbolic hands, roots, typography, African-inspired pattern work and the raised megaphone concept that ultimately became the foundation of the final mark.

02 / Proofing

Chosen Direction

Narrowing It Down

Two developed directions were presented side by side. The megaphone concept moved forward, giving the identity a stronger sense of voice, leadership, energy and movement.

03 / Final

Final Logo

The Finished Identity

The selected direction combines the illustrated raised megaphone with the Manhood Basic Training wordmark and pattern system as one complete vertical mark.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

ARTIST53 respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use artist53.com, why it is collected and how it may be handled.

Information You Provide

You may choose to provide information such as your name, email address, company or brand, project details, budget range and other information you submit through contact or email signup forms.

Email Signup

If you subscribe to the ARTIST53 email list, your signup information is processed through MailerLite so ARTIST53 can send updates, new work, releases and shop news. You can unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in marketing emails.

How Information Is Used

  • To respond to questions and project inquiries.
  • To provide requested services or information.
  • To send email updates when you have subscribed.
  • To operate, maintain and improve the website.
  • To protect the website and prevent misuse when reasonably necessary.

Third Party Services

ARTIST53 may use third party services to operate parts of the website and communicate with visitors. MailerLite is used for email signup and email marketing. FormSubmit is used to process contact-form inquiries and deliver them to ARTIST53 by email; submitted names, contact information and project details are processed by FormSubmit for this purpose. FormSubmit may use spam-protection checks. When you interact with a third party service, that provider may process information according to its own privacy practices.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Third party services embedded on or connected to the website may use cookies or similar technologies as part of their operation. Your browser settings may allow you to limit or block cookies.

Information Sharing

ARTIST53 does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when needed to operate the website, deliver requested services, comply with legal obligations or protect the rights and security of ARTIST53 and its users.

Data Retention

Information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining business records and meeting legal obligations.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time. You may also contact ARTIST53 to ask about personal information you have submitted through the website or to request that it be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated as the website, services or privacy practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email justin@artist53.com.

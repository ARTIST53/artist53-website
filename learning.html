ARTIST53 Logo Learning / Stage 01

What Makes A Great Logo?

A great logo does more than look good. It gives people a clear, recognizable symbol for the business behind it. The strongest logos are simple, memorable, readable, versatile, relevant and unique.

↔Swipe on mobile or use the arrows on desktop
01 / Overview

A Logo Must Communicate And Perform

A strong logo identifies the business, supports the right impression and remains useful wherever the brand needs to appear.

Test Your Logo: Can someone recognize and understand its central idea quickly?

02 / Simple

Simple Does Not Mean Empty. It Means Focused.

Removing unnecessary details makes the main idea easier to recognize, reproduce and remember.

Test Your Logo: What can be removed without losing the idea?

View Trinity Telecom →
03 / Memorable

Give People Something Distinctive To Remember.

A recognizable silhouette, visual idea or personality helps a logo stay in the mind after the first encounter.

Test Your Logo: Could someone describe it after seeing it briefly?

View K-9 Kreedentials →
04 / Readable

The Name Must Be Clear At A Glance.

Custom typography can have personality without forcing the audience to work to understand the brand name.

Test Your Logo: Can it be read quickly on a phone, sign or printed piece?

View CLIK CLIK BANG →
05 / Versatile

A Great Logo Must Survive The Real World.

It should work large or small, in full color or one color, and as a complete logo or approved alternate version.

Test Your Logo: Does it still work small, black-and-white and without special effects?

View Manhood Basic Training →
06 / Relevant

Fit The Audience, Purpose And Personality.

The visual language should feel appropriate for the people the business serves and the experience it promises.

Test Your Logo: Does this feel right for this specific business and audience?

View Alexander Photos →
07 / Unique

Create An Idea The Brand Can Own.

A custom concept gives the business a stronger identity than a familiar template or generic industry symbol.

Test Your Logo: Could it be mistaken for a competitor's logo?

View Marquis Grissom →
08 / Logo Scorecard

Does Your Logo Pass The Six Tests?

  1. Simple: Is the main idea focused?
  2. Memorable: Is there something distinctive to remember?
  3. Readable: Is the name clear?
  4. Versatile: Does it work everywhere?
  5. Relevant: Does it fit the audience?
  6. Unique: Can the brand own the idea?
08 / Apply The Lesson

A logo does not need to perform every test perfectly on day one. The scorecard shows where refinement can make it stronger.

Mascot Identity

A Character Built To Carry The Brand

Hero Da Barber needed more than a wordmark. The project called for a friendly, recognizable mascot that could work as a full character, a compact icon and a supporting logo system across apparel, social media and promotional materials.

ClientHero Da Barber
IndustryBarbering & Personal Brand
ServicesMascot Design & Logo System
DeliverablesFull Mascot, Icons & Alternate Marks

The System

One Character. Multiple Uses.

The full mascot gives the brand personality, while the smaller icon and letter based marks make the identity easier to use at reduced sizes. Together they create a flexible system instead of a single image.

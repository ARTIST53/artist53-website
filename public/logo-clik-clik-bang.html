Logo Identity Case Study

CLIK CLIK BANG

I had already worked with this client through Trinity Telecom. When he moved into a completely different business and opened CLIK CLIK BANG in Arizona, he came back to me for the new identity. The project grew from the core logo into storefront branding, promotional graphics, social media campaigns and event merchandise.

Bang N Buy

The identity expanded into the Bang N Buy event campaign. I created promotional artwork for social media and developed event apparel so the campaign carried one consistent visual language from the screen to the event itself.

Range Officer Shirt System

The event branding also moved onto apparel. I designed the Range Officer system with a dedicated RSO emblem and finished front and back shirt graphics.

05 / Complete System

From Logo To Brand Experience

CLIK CLIK BANG became more than a single logo project. The identity moved across storefront signage, launch advertising, social graphics, event promotion and apparel—showing how one strong visual system can keep a growing brand recognizable across every touchpoint.

01 / Exploration

Sketches & Early Ideas

Building The Attitude

Bombshell Boulevard began with loose hand-drawn exploration focused on a bold, feminine and fashion-forward personality. The goal was to create a mark with enough edge to match the brand while still feeling stylish and recognizable.

Locking In The Look

The selected direction pushed the custom script forward as the personality of the mark while the condensed Boulevard lettering and distressed brick treatment gave the identity structure, texture and an urban fashion edge.

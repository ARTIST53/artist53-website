Logo Identity Case Study

CLIK CLIK BANG

I had already worked with this client through Trinity Telecom. When he moved into a completely different business and opened CLIK CLIK BANG in Arizona, he came back to me for the new identity. The project grew from the core logo into storefront branding, promotional graphics, social media campaigns and the Bang N Buy event campaign.

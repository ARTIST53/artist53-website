Logo Identity Case Study

CLIK CLIK BANG

I had already worked with this client through Trinity Telecom. When he moved into a completely different business and opened CLIK CLIK BANG in Arizona, he came back to me for the new identity. This time the brand needed to feel bold, direct and strong enough to work on a storefront, in ads and on its own as a symbol.

01 / The Client

A New Business

From Trinity Telecom To CLIK CLIK BANG

This was not a new client relationship. We had already worked together on Trinity Telecom, so there was trust in the process. When the business changed, the identity had to change with it. CLIK CLIK BANG needed its own look and its own attitude for a Black owned gun store in Arizona.

02 / The Identity

The Logo

The CLIK CLIK BANG Identity

The logo is built around the CCB initials, with the circular C and the B locking together into one mark. The target detail sits at the center, while the full version carries the CLIK CLIK BANG name and the firearms line underneath. Red, black and white became the core look.

03 / Real World Use

Built For The Store

More Than A Logo File

The mark had to work bigger than a screen. It needed to read on the building, in a small social post and across the graphics customers would actually see. The opening day piece shows the logo working with the real storefront while keeping the same red, black and white look.

04 / Marketing

Store Graphics

Marketing The New Store

Once the identity was in place, I carried it into the store graphics. Opening day, classes, membership offers and promotions all used the same red, black and white system so the brand stayed consistent.

CLIK CLIK BANG

I had already worked with this client through Trinity Telecom. When he moved into the firearms business and opened CLIK CLIK BANG in Arizona, he came back to me for the new identity. 02 / The Identity

The Logo

The CLIK CLIK BANG Identity

The CCB initials became the main symbol. The target sits in the center of the mark and the full logo adds the CLIK CLIK BANG name with FIREARMS underneath. Red, black and white gave the store a strong visual system that could be repeated everywhere.

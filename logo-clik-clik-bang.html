Brand Evolution Case Study

CLIK CLIK BANG

A long-time ARTIST53 client entered a completely new chapter—from telecommunications into owning and operating a Black-owned firearms store in Arizona. The new business needed an identity with enough impact to work as a standalone symbol, a full retail logo, storefront signage and launch marketing.

01 / Client Evolution

A New Business Chapter

From Telecom To Firearms

The relationship began years earlier through Trinity Telecom. When the client transitioned into a new retail venture in Arizona, ARTIST53 was brought forward with him to establish the visual identity for CLIK CLIK BANG. The goal was to create a brand that felt bold, memorable and immediately recognizable across physical and promotional applications.

Logo Identity Case Study

CLIK CLIK BANG

This logo was created after I previously worked with the client on Trinity Telecom. When they launched CLIK CLIK BANG in Arizona, they brought me back to develop the new identity. What started with the logo eventually grew into a full visual system used throughout the store, marketing, social media, packaging, merchandise and events.

01 / Identity

Final Logo System

The final logo system includes three versions of the identity: CLIK CLIK Bang Firearms, CLIK CLIK Bang and the iconic B symbol. Each version was created in red, black and white so the brand could work across different backgrounds and applications.

02 / Brand Application

Logos In Use

The logo was designed to work beyond the screen. It was used throughout the brand on storefront signage, packaging and other real world applications.

03 / Marketing

Store Opening Campaign

For the store opening, I created a printed flyer along with a series of social media graphics to help introduce the brand and promote the opening.

Store Opening Social Graphics
04 / Event Campaign

Bang N Buy

The CLIK CLIK BANG identity was also used to promote the Bang N Buy event. I created graphics for the event announcement, food trucks, raffles, featured guests, vendors and sponsors.
